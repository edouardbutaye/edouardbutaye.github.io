Research experience

  • Current

    Postdoctoral researcher: hybrid RANS-LES modelling

    CEA Saclay, France · 2025 – present · with A. Burlot

    Implementation of the Hybrid Temporal Large Eddy Simulation (HTLES) method of Rémi Manceau in TRUST/Trio_CFD (C++), where a turbulent forcing term is added to the momentum equation to address the grey-area problem in the RANS-to-LES transition. The goal is a mesh-independent physical criterion, built from RANS quantities alone, for deciding where to refine towards LES. Validated on the backward-facing step, periodic hill and T-junction. GPU porting with Kokkos.

  • PhD in fluid mechanics: particle-resolved DNS of anisothermal fluid–solid flows

    PROMES – CNRS, Perpignan, France · 2021 – 2024 · with A. Toutant, S. Mer, F. Bataille

    Particle-Resolved Direct Numerical Simulation of anisothermal fluidized beds of more than 8 500 solid particles, using a one-fluid formulation coupled to the Front-Tracking method, developed in TRUST/Trio_CFD (C++) on more than 3 800 CPUs. Lagrangian and Eulerian statistics computed with Pandas; post-processing with GNU Parallel, VisIt and PyVista. Included 92 h of teaching and the supervision of three interns.

  • Master 2 internship: multiscale simulations of horizontal boiling flows

    PROMES – CNRS, Perpignan, France · 2021 (6 months) · with A. Toutant, S. Mer, F. Bataille

    Study of horizontal turbulent boiling two-phase flows in concentrated solar power plants driven by direct steam generation, using Neptune_CFD coupled to Syrthes to resolve conduction in the receiving tube wall.
