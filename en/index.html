Research and CFD solver development for turbulent and multiphase flows

I work on the numerical simulation of turbulent and multiphase flows, across the whole chain: the physical analysis of a flow, the model that describes it, its implementation in production CFD software, and its validation against experiment.

Download my CV (PDF)

Now Postdoctoral researcher at CEA Saclay, implementing the hybrid RANS-LES method HTLES in TrioCFD and applying it to internal turbulent flows, with Alan Burlot.

Before that, I completed my PhD in 2024 at the PROMES laboratory of the University of Perpignan Via Domitia, on particle-resolved simulation of anisothermal fluid-particle flows for concentrated solar power, under the supervision of Adrien Toutant, Samuel Mer and Françoise Bataille.

Research interests

High-fidelity simulation is still out of reach for most industrial configurations, while the models used in its place are usually calibrated on academic cases far removed from the ones they are applied to. What interests me is closing that distance: deciding how much of a flow genuinely has to be resolved, and recovering what a coarse computation leaves out instead of accepting the error. Hybrid RANS/LES formulations and subgrid corrections are two routes I have taken so far, and I would like to keep working on the question they share, which is what makes an affordable computation trustworthy.

Research

  • Hybrid RANS-LES simulation

    Implementation of the HTLES method in TrioCFD, and definition of a physical criterion to locate the regions of the domain that require an unsteady description.

  • Particle-resolved simulation

    Hydrodynamics and heat transfer in anisothermal fluidized beds, and a subgrid-corrected resolution scale for simulations that cannot afford to resolve the boundary layers.

  • Two-phase boiling flows

    Euler-Euler simulation of boiling flows in the receiver tubes of direct steam generation plants, with conjugate heat transfer in the tube wall.

All research activities

Software

My developments go into TRUST/Trio_CFD, the open-source CFD platform of CEA, where they become available to everyone else using the code. During my PhD I added the computation of the hydrodynamic forces and of the heat flux on resolved particles, an optimised collision detection combining the Link-Cell method with Verlet tables that cut the overall computation time by 20%, a more accurate phase indicator at the faces and edges of the control volumes, and the subgrid correction of the hydrodynamic force at coarse resolution. The current work adds the HTLES formulation and its turbulence forcing term.

Each of these comes with validation forms and non-regression test cases, without which a contribution does not survive contact with the next release. I have also worked with NEPTUNE_CFD, Code_Saturne, SYRTHES, Salomé and the MMG remesher, in C++ and Python, on national supercomputers running up to 3 800 processors, and on GPU porting through Kokkos.
