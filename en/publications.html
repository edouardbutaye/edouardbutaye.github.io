  • Journal article

    Extensive analysis of hydrodynamics in liquid–solid fluidized beds with particle-resolved simulations

    E. Butaye, S. Mer, F. Bataille, A. Toutant

    International Journal of Multiphase Flow · 202 (2026) 105789

    Particle-resolved simulations of a liquid–solid fluidized bed, analyzing the mechanisms that generate turbulence. Extends the correlation of Alméras et al. (2019) to predict the fluid velocity variance.

  • Journal article

    Investigation of heat transfers with particle-resolved simulations: from Stokes flow to fluidized bed

    E. Butaye, R. Quintana, S. Mer, F. Bataille, A. Toutant

    International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer · 241 (2025) 126687

    Particle-resolved simulations of fluid–particle and wall-to-bed heat transfer, from a settling particle in Stokes flow to an anisothermal fluidized bed. Heat transfer proves far more grid-sensitive than bed expansion.

    PDF DOI

  • PhD thesis

    Modélisation et simulations résolues d'écoulement fluide-particules : du régime de Stokes aux lits fluidisés anisothermes

    E. Butaye · supervised by A. Toutant, S. Mer, F. Bataille

    Université de Perpignan Via Domitia · defended 9 December 2024 · in French

    Particle-resolved direct numerical simulation of anisothermal fluid–particle flows for solar receivers, covering fluidized bed hydrodynamics and wall heat transfer. Introduces the PR-SCS subgrid-corrected resolution scale.

    PDF

  • Journal article

    Development of Particle Resolved – Subgrid Corrected Simulations: hydrodynamic force calculation and flow sub-resolution corrections

    E. Butaye, A. Toutant, S. Mer, F. Bataille

    Computers & Fluids · 267 (2023) 106071

    Quantifies how far hydrodynamic forces are under-resolved at ten cells per particle diameter, then builds a mesh-dependent correction. The resulting PR-SCS approach is assessed over Reynolds numbers and density ratios.

    PDF DOI

  • Journal article

    Euler–Euler multi-scale simulations of internal boiling flow with conjugated heat transfer

    E. Butaye, A. Toutant, S. Mer

    Applied Mechanics · 4 (2023) 191–209

    Euler–Euler two-fluid simulations of boiling R141b in a horizontal serpentine tube, coupling NEPTUNE_CFD to SYRTHES for wall conduction. Shows strong coupling between the solid temperature field and the flow regimes.
