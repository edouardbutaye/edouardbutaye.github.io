Teaching

During my PhD I applied for teaching positions at the University of Perpignan Via Domitia and at the Sup'ENR engineering school, in order to gain a first experience in teaching. I taught tutorial classes and laboratory sessions in fluid mechanics, radiative transfer, numerical methods and wind energy production, for a total of 92 hours between 2022 and 2024. These courses are part of the Master Energie Solaire of the University of Perpignan and of the engineering curriculum of Sup'ENR.

Courses

  • Fluid mechanics and heat transfer

    Master 1, Master Energie Solaire · 24 h tutorial classes, 12 h laboratory sessions · 2022 to 2024

    Tutorial classes on the computation of the Nusselt number for flows over flat plates using correlations, and on the computation of the dynamic and thermal boundary layer thicknesses. Laboratory sessions on Bernoulli's theorem and pressure losses.

  • Advanced radiative transfer

    Master 2, Master Energie Solaire · 13.5 h tutorial classes · 2022 to 2023

    Definition, computation and exercises on the basic properties: luminance, emittance, radiosity, form factors, grey and black bodies, and the electrical analogy. Matlab project on the optimisation of the radiative transfer towards a solar receiver.

  • Numerical tools and PDEs for solar energy

    Master 1 and Licence 3, Master Energie Solaire and Sup'ENR · 18 h tutorial classes · 2023 to 2024

    Discretisation of partial differential equations using the finite difference method.

  • Renewable energy technologies 2

    Master 1, Sup'ENR · 12 h tutorial classes · 2022 to 2024

    Exercises and development of a Python code based on the blade element theory, for the computation of the lift, the drag and the axial thrust. The code is then used within a project to study the lift, thrust and drag of a NACA airfoil.

  • Energy sources and conversion modes

    Licence 3, Sup'ENR · 12 h tutorial classes · 2022 to 2024

    Exercises on the assessment of the production of a wind turbine with the WindPro software. The students write a detailed report presenting the method and the results obtained.
